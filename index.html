Why use FinanceCalculatorsHub?

FinanceCalculatorsHub gives you a reliable, transparent and fast way to plan loans, investments and withdrawals. Each tool uses standard financial formulas and provides a downloadable PDF report you can attach to client notes, tax files, or personal records.

What you get from each calculator

  • EMI: Monthly payment, amortization schedule, total interest paid and visual summaries.
  • SIP: Future value projection, CAGR equivalent, monthly contributions and total contribution breakdown.
  • Lumpsum: Future value with compounding frequency, and clear assumptions for inflation-adjusted outcomes.
  • SWP: Withdrawal planning with capital preservation checks and timeline simulation.
  • FD: Maturity value, interest compounded frequency, and yearly breakdown.
  • RD: Recurring deposit maturity calculation and total interest earned.
  • CD: Certificate of Deposit maturity value, APY calculation and interest earned.
  • Mortgage: Monthly payment, amortization schedule with dates, taxes, insurance and PMI.

Precision and privacy

All calculations run in the user's browser. Results are rounded for display but the underlying computations use full precision. We do not store inputs by default.
